# Purpose

This document defines the commercial strategy, business model, market positioning, pricing strategy, customer journey, and long-term growth plan for the HImpact Digital Event Automation Platform.

---

# Product Overview

HImpact is a Digital Event Automation Platform that transforms how events are planned, experienced, and remembered.

Rather than providing only an invitation website, HImpact delivers a complete digital ecosystem that supports organizers before, during, and after the event.

The first product released to market targets weddings while using a generic event engine capable of supporting future event types.

---

# Vision Statement

Build the most trusted Digital Event Automation Platform in the Middle East before expanding globally.

---

# Value Proposition

HImpact provides organizers with a single platform to:

- Create beautiful digital invitations.
- Automate guest engagement.
- Collect memories from every guest.
- Store media securely.
- Deliver a premium event experience.
- Reduce manual coordination.

Guests enjoy a frictionless experience without installing an application.

# Pricing Strategy

Hybrid pricing model.

Customers choose a predefined package and may purchase optional upgrades.

Example packages

| Package | Guests | Photos / Guest | Price |
|----------|--------|----------------|-------|
| Starter | 100 | 30 | 200 EGP |
| Standard | 200 | 30 | 300 EGP |
| Premium | Configurable | Configurable | TBD |

Optional upgrades

- Additional guests
- Additional storage
- Additional photos
- Premium themes
- Custom branding
- Concierge setup

Pricing is configurable through the administration portal and never hardcoded.
